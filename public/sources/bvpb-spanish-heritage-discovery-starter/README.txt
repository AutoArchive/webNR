Purpose
-------
This WebNR-maintained source is a small link-only discovery directory for the
Biblioteca Virtual del Patrimonio Bibliografico (BVPB), operated by Spain's
Ministry of Culture. It helps readers reach the official collection, its search
surface, and its current rights guidance without mirroring catalog records,
scans, PDFs, JPEG files, OCR, OAI/SRU responses, or other collection data.

Origin identity and reader value
--------------------------------
BVPB is managed by the Ministry of Culture's library-coordination service and
aggregates digitized bibliographic heritage held by libraries, archives, museums,
universities, foundations, academies, and other participating institutions. Its
current first-party presentation describes open and free access to more than
36,000 printed books, more than 10,000 manuscripts, more than 5,000 cartographic
materials, roughly 85,000 graphic and photographic materials, more than 800
scores, and additional formats.

The three entries in this starter are deliberately navigation records:

- the official BVPB collection home page;
- the first-party advanced search surface;
- the first-party legal and reuse guidance.

Rights and reuse boundary
-------------------------
BVPB's current legal notice distinguishes the copyright status of underlying
works from the terms attached to digital reproductions. It says that the
underlying works are mostly public domain, while the descriptive record for each
digital object carries the authoritative reuse license. The same notice says the
majority of BVPB digital works are offered under CC BY 4.0, but some works remain
subject to rights or use different and more restrictive licenses.

WebNR therefore does not turn open access, age, or a majority-license statement
into a corpus-wide redistribution claim. It stores only three reviewed
first-party URLs plus WebNR-authored labels and descriptions. Readers should use
the license attached to the exact BVPB record when they need reuse beyond normal
reading or linking.

Current WebNR behavior
----------------------
Adding this source downloads only WebNR's small static YAML index. Selecting an
entry opens the corresponding BVPB page in the browser. WebNR performs no runtime
BVPB search, crawl, API/OAI/SRU request, document download, OCR request,
background synchronization, credential forwarding, or proxying for this starter.

This source is discovery-only. It does not claim that BVPB scans or PDFs can be
imported directly into WebNR's TXT reader and it does not copy BVPB metadata or
rights statements into a local catalog.

Audit boundary and future upgrade
---------------------------------
The BVPB first-party presentation, search page, FAQ, and legal notice were
reviewed on 2026-09-17. The admitted pages were reachable without login for
ordinary discovery and rights-context reading. The Ministry of Culture also
identifies a bounded set of 6,769 older Registro de la Propiedad Intelectual
works as public domain; that evidence was used only to confirm that item or
collection-level rights can be explicit and was not generalized to the whole
BVPB corpus.

A future content adapter would require a separate source-specific audit of exact
item endpoints, record-level licenses and attribution, OAI/SRU terms, browser
transport/CORS, file identity, update/deletion semantics, pagination/cadence,
resource/failure limits, supported formats, and versioned fixtures. No such
capability is claimed by this starter.
