Purpose
-------
This WebNR-maintained source is a small link-only discovery directory for the
current German-language Projekt Gutenberg at projekt-gutenberg.org. It helps
readers reach the official reading portal, the title catalogue, and the current
terms of use without copying, transforming, mirroring, or redistributing the
site-provided literary texts, EPUB files, editorial material, or metadata.

Origin identity and reader value
--------------------------------
Projekt Gutenberg presents German-language classical literature as readable,
searchable full text and, since June 2026, offers EPUB downloads for books on the
new site. The project states that the literary works are made available as public
domain to the best of its knowledge and that the platform has been under new
responsibility since 1 January 2026.

The three entries in this starter are deliberately reader-navigation and rights
evidence routes:

- the official Projekt Gutenberg home and reading entry point;
- the first-party A-Z title catalogue;
- the current first-party terms of use.

Rights and reuse boundary
-------------------------
The current first-party terms reviewed on 2026-09-20 say that literary works are
provided as public domain to the best of the site's knowledge, while also limiting
use of the texts supplied on the website to private use and prohibiting broader
reproduction, redistribution, publication, adaptation, or other use beyond that
private-use boundary. The site's editorial preparation, structure, explanatory
text, metadata, and design are separately protected unless stated otherwise.

WebNR therefore does not treat the public-domain status of an underlying work as
permission to republish the site's particular text or EPUB edition. This source
stores only three reviewed first-party URLs plus WebNR-authored labels and
descriptions. It does not ingest or proxy book content.

Current WebNR behavior
----------------------
Adding this source downloads only WebNR's small static YAML index. Selecting an
entry opens the corresponding first-party page in the browser. WebNR performs no
runtime Projekt Gutenberg search, chapter scraping, full-text extraction, EPUB
download, metadata harvest, background synchronization, credential forwarding,
or proxying for this starter.

Audit boundary and future upgrade
---------------------------------
The current home page, title catalogue, June 2026 EPUB announcement, representative
book pages, and terms of use were reviewed on 2026-09-20. A future richer adapter
would require a separate source-specific audit establishing explicit permission
for the intended automated use plus stable record/file identity, browser
transport, update/deletion semantics, pagination, request/resource limits,
encoding, provenance, attribution, failure behavior, and versioned fixtures.
No such runtime capability is claimed by this starter.
