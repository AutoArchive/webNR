Purpose
-------
This WebNR-maintained source is a small link-only discovery directory for the
Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes (BVMC), the University of Alicante's
Digital Humanities Center and online library for Spanish and Ibero-American
literature and culture. It helps readers reach the official library, its
literature area, and its Biblioteca Americana area while leaving reading,
search, catalog records, media, and downloadable files at the first-party site.

Origin identity and reader value
--------------------------------
BVMC describes its mission as making relevant works from Hispanic cultures
freely accessible worldwide and provides a large digital catalog spanning
literature, language, history, archives, periodicals, and other collections.
Its literature area exposes thousands of digitized works across narrative,
theatre, poetry, criticism, and literary history, while Biblioteca Americana
provides a dedicated route into Ibero-American collections.

The three entries in this starter are deliberately navigation records rather
than copies of the origin catalog:

- the BVMC home/catalog entry point for broad discovery;
- the first-party Literature area for Spanish and Ibero-American literature;
- the first-party Biblioteca Americana area for American collections.

Rights and reuse boundary
-------------------------
BVMC's current legal framework states that the site and its protected content
remain subject to intellectual-property terms. It authorizes users to view,
print, copy, or store protected literary works and other content for personal
and private use, but says other uses, including distribution or online
dissemination, require express written authorization. Individual records can
also describe their own source edition, format, origin institution, or rights
status, so free access must not be treated as a uniform redistribution license.

WebNR therefore does not mirror BVMC text, HTML/PDF files, audio, video,
images, MARC/RDF records, search results, catalog metadata, biographies,
editorial material, or collection descriptions. This starter stores only
first-party destination URLs plus short WebNR-authored labels and descriptions.
Work-, edition-, object-, and collection-specific rights remain at the origin.

Current WebNR behavior
----------------------
Selecting an item opens its official BVMC page. WebNR performs no runtime BVMC
HTML, search, MARC, RDF, PDF, API, image, audio, video, or catalog polling for
this starter. It does not proxy downloads, automate accounts, send credentials
or cookies, scrape search results, or reproduce origin metadata. The existing
WebNR reader can still import local or otherwise supported text separately;
this source makes no claim that WebNR can parse BVMC-hosted formats.

Audit boundary and future upgrade
---------------------------------
The public home page, Literature area, Biblioteca Americana area, service
information, and legal framework were reviewed on 2026-09-13. Because the
admitted runtime is link-only, CORS, robots behavior, request cadence,
pagination, response-size ceilings, catalog/API schemas, and media transport are
outside this source's current runtime dependency: WebNR does not make those
automated requests.

A future search, catalog, linked-data, or document adapter would require a
separate audit of the exact endpoint, access policy, transport behavior,
item-level rights statements, update/deletion semantics, pagination, timeouts,
response-size ceilings, provenance, attribution, privacy, credential needs, and
versioned fixtures. It must not infer that a freely readable page or a
public-domain underlying work grants permission to redistribute every BVMC
digital edition or associated asset.
