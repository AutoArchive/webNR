Purpose
-------
This WebNR-maintained source is a small first-party link-only discovery directory
for literary material in Memoria Chilena, the digital cultural-heritage portal of
the Biblioteca Nacional de Chile. It helps readers reach the official literature
topic surface and the portal's own rights guidance without copying, transforming,
mirroring, or redistributing scans, images, PDFs, audiovisual files, RDF records,
editorial minisitios, or other digital objects.

Origin identity and reader value
--------------------------------
Memoria Chilena describes itself as a digital resource centre built from
digitised documents in the collections of the Biblioteca Nacional de Chile and
other institutions of Chile's Servicio Nacional del Patrimonio Cultural. Its
literature topic surface currently brings together hundreds of minisitios across
authors, literary criticism, genres, reading and publishing history, and related
books, periodicals, photographs, manuscripts, and other documents.

The three entries in this starter are deliberately reader-navigation and rights
evidence routes:

- the official literature topic and discovery page;
- the first-party project/about page with copyright guidance;
- the first-party FAQ covering intellectual-property, citation, and reproduction
  questions.

Rights and reuse boundary
-------------------------
The first-party project description reviewed on 2026-09-21 says Memoria Chilena
prioritises digitising works in the public domain or Chile's patrimonio cultural
común. That is not a collection-wide licence. Representative current records
explicitly distinguish material marked `Patrimonio cultural común`, which the
site says may be used and reproduced freely, from `Obra protegida`, whose use and
reproduction require author or rights-holder permission. The website platform,
design, and portal-authored editorial components are separately protected.

WebNR therefore does not infer redistribution rights from free online access,
from a download button, or from a collection-level description. This starter
stores only three reviewed first-party URLs plus WebNR-authored labels and
descriptions. It does not ingest or proxy document content or metadata.

Current WebNR behavior
----------------------
Adding this source downloads only WebNR's small static YAML index. Selecting an
entry opens the corresponding first-party Memoria Chilena page in the browser.
WebNR performs no runtime search against Memoria Chilena, no RDF or metadata
harvest, no image/scan/PDF/audio/video fetch, no full-text extraction, no
background synchronization, no credential forwarding, and no proxying for this
starter.

Audit boundary and future upgrade
---------------------------------
The literature topic, project/about page, copyright FAQ, a representative
`Patrimonio cultural común` record, and representative `Obra protegida` records
were reviewed on 2026-09-21. A future richer adapter would require a separate
source-specific audit establishing the intended automated-use permission and
item-level rights propagation, stable record/file identity, browser transport,
update/deletion semantics, pagination, request/resource limits, encoding,
provenance, attribution, failure behavior, and versioned fixtures. No such
runtime capability is claimed by this starter.
