Purpose
-------
This starter gives readers a bounded route into AliceAndBooks' curated classic
library. AliceAndBooks states on its first-party library pages that every title
in the library is public domain in the United States or published under an open
license, with no sign-up required and DRM-free PDF/EPUB downloads. Individual
book pages also state the applicable U.S. public-domain status and warn readers
that copyright terms can differ outside the United States.

The starter contains the official library plus three representative first-party
book pages:

- Pride and Prejudice — Jane Austen
- Frankenstein — Mary Shelley
- White Nights — Fyodor Dostoevsky, in the Constance Garnett translation named by the origin

Rights and access boundary
--------------------------
AliceAndBooks' public-domain statements support ordinary reader discovery, but
the site's current Terms of Use impose a different boundary on the website and
its site content. Those terms prohibit automated or non-human access, systematic
data retrieval for a collection/database/directory, and data-mining or robot
activity, while granting ordinary site use on limited terms.

WebNR therefore does not crawl AliceAndBooks, mirror its files, synchronize its
catalog, copy its descriptions, covers, rankings, download counts, reading-time
estimates, recommendations, or other site metadata. The entries below are a
small manually reviewed directory made from explicit first-party destination
URLs plus WebNR-authored labels and descriptions. Their purpose is to let a
reader navigate to the origin and make an informed choice there.

Why this is discovery-only
--------------------------
This capability is link discovery rather than direct PDF/EPUB/TXT import. The
reason is not a claim that the underlying public-domain works are restricted.
It is the combination of AliceAndBooks' current site-access terms and WebNR's
source-audit rule against turning an ordinary reader surface into an automated
catalog interface without source-specific permission and fixtures.

A later richer integration would require a first-party interface or explicit
permission whose machine-access scope, cadence, identity, update/deletion,
resource, attribution, and failure semantics can be tested without contradicting
the current Terms of Use. Until then, link-only is the truthful capability.

Runtime boundary
----------------
Adding this source fetches only WebNR's small static YAML index. Opening an entry
navigates to the first-party AliceAndBooks library or book page. WebNR performs no
background polling, origin crawl, bulk download, API call, server-side proxy,
account or credential use, cookie automation, JavaScript execution, CAPTCHA
handling, robots bypass, or access-control bypass.

Identity, update, deletion, and failure behavior
-----------------------------------------------
- Stable identity: the explicit AliceAndBooks library URL or reviewed book-page URL.
- Content transport: none in this capability level; discovery links only.
- Pagination: none; four explicit entries are maintained by WebNR.
- Request cadence: no polling or synchronization.
- Updates: a later operating cycle may revise the bounded list only after a new
  source-specific audit and normal PR/CI/public verification.
- Deletions: an upstream omission never silently removes or replaces an entry.
- Failure: an unavailable or redirected origin is reported through source-health
  review rather than silently replaced by a mirror.
